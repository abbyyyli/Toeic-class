常見陷阱速查

對照容易誤選的形式與正確搭配，回到原題時要同時看空格的前後線索。

答案與考點速查 · 101–121

題號答案核心規則

錯誤形式 → 正確形式

101 · 配對片語
✗ involves creating … but also deciding
✓ involves creating … as well as deciding

but also 通常須與 not only 配對。

102 · 固定句型
✗ have no choice and to V
✓ have no choice but to V

no choice 的排除意思要用 but to V。

103 · 動名詞與受詞
✗ for celebration George’s promotion
✓ for celebrating George’s promotion

celebration 後若接受詞需改成 a celebration of。

104 · 所有格關係詞
✗ a provider who patients…
✓ a provider whose patients…

空格後緊接 patients，須用 whose 表所屬。

106 · 片語動詞
✗ delayed us up
✓ held us up

delay us 雖可，但原句已有 up。

107 · 過去遺憾
✗ wished she will follow
✓ wished she had followed

對過去未發生之事的遺憾用 wish + had p.p.。

109 · 介系詞搭配
✗ additionally from teaching
✓ aside from teaching

空格後的 from 決定片語是 aside from。

112 / 121 · 動詞受詞
✗ encourage / enable someone work
✓ encourage / enable someone to work

這兩個動詞接受詞後，動作用 to V。
