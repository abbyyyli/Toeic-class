6動詞題六步驟

看到動詞題，照這個流程跑
Step 1
找主詞：是誰？單數還是複數？（決定加不加 s、is/are、was/were、has/have）
Step 2
找時間線索：yesterday/ago → 過去；tomorrow/next week → 未來；since/for/over the past → 完成式；while/when + 過去 → 過去進行
Step 3
看空格前有沒有助動詞（have/has/had/be/will/should…），有的話後面形式被鎖定
Step 4
判斷主動或被動：主詞是做動作，還是接受動作？
Step 5
判斷簡單／進行／完成／完成進行：一次動作？正在做？已完成連到現在？比另一個過去更早？
Step 6
檢查固定搭配：be scheduled to / suggest that + 原形 / would rather + 原形 等

🪤動詞與時態常見錯誤

第三人稱單數現在式加 s
✅ Jerry leaves at 8.
❌ Jerry leave at 8.
完成式用過去分詞
✅ He has gone.
❌ He has went.
明確過去時間用過去式
✅ I saw him yesterday.
❌ I have seen him yesterday.
since 要現在完成式
✅ Since 1995, he has owned the firm.
❌ Since 1995, he owns the firm.
sales 自己上升，用 rise
✅ Sales have risen.
❌ Sales have been raising.
found = 創立，p.p. 是 founded
✅ The company was founded in 2001.
❌ The company was found in 2001.
被動要 be + p.p.
✅ The report was written by John.
❌ The report written by John.
did 後接原形
✅ I did try.
❌ I did tried.
時間子句用現在式代替未來
✅ When he returns, I will call you.
❌ When he will return, I will call you.
know 通常不用進行式
✅ She knows the answer.
❌ She is knowing the answer.
happen 是不及物動詞，不能用被動
✅ The meeting happened yesterday.
❌ The meeting was happened yesterday.
