Lesson 07 · Chapter 5 形容詞與比較結構

建立比較句的判斷系統 🧭

重點不是背 big/bigger/biggest，而是看到空格能判斷：這裡要形容詞、副詞、比較級、最高級，還是固定片語。看到 than 想比較級、看到 as…as 中間放原級、看到 prior 後面接 to。

1形容詞的四個位置

先判斷空格在句子裡做什麼
看到空格要放形容詞還是副詞？先看它的位置。
位置結構例
1名詞前 Adj+Na reliable employee
2be 動詞後The employee is reliable.
3連綴動詞後 seem/look/remainThe plan sounds practical.
4受詞補語found the report useful
常見錯誤：The manager is carefully → careful；needs an efficiently system → efficient。be / seem 後面用形容詞，不是副詞。
❓ 空格在名詞前面、或在 be / seem / remain 後面，通常要什麼詞性？
形容詞（不是副詞）
❓ The assistant seems capable. capable 修飾誰？
修飾主詞 assistant，是主詞補語

2原級 / 比較級 / 最高級

三層概念與判斷線索
怎麼判斷要用哪一層？看句子有沒有比較線索。
形式用途判斷線索
原單純描述無 than / 範圍 / as…as
比兩者比較常見 than
最範圍內最the + 最高級 + in/of/among
有 than → 比較級；有 in the city / of the three / among all → 最高級。
❓ 看到 than 第一個反應是什麼？
往前找比較級，並確認在比哪兩個東西
❓ This is ___ solution of the three. → 用哪一層？
最高級 the most practical（of the three 是範圍）

3字形變化：-er/-est vs more/most

短加字尾、長用 more/most
類型比較級最高級
短（fast, large, easy, big）faster / larger / easier / biggerfastest / largest / easiest / biggest
長（important, efficient, reliable）more important…the most important…
兩種比較級不可混用：more easier → easier；more expensiver → more expensive。
❓ important 的比較級怎麼說？
more important（長形容詞不加 -er）
❓ more easier 錯在哪？
easier 已是比較級，前面不能再加 more

4不規則變化 + far / late 辨析

TOEIC 必背
原級比較級最高級
good / wellbetterbest
badworseworst
many / muchmoremost
littlelessleast
farfarther（距離）/ further（進一步）farthest / furthest
TOEIC 最常考：further information / further assistance / further discussion（抽象「進一步」，不是 farther）。better 已是比較級 → 不說 more better。
❓ We have ___ time than expected. (little/less/least)
less（有 than 是比較級，little→less）
❓ 「進一步的資訊」用 farther 還是 further？
further information

5比較級 + than

含程度副詞
A + be/V + 比較級 + than + B
程度副詞用法
much / far / even / slightly / significantly放比較級前，表差距大小
very只修飾原級（very good）
陷阱：very better → much/far better；very more expensive → far more expensive。
❓ much larger 和 slightly higher 差別？
差距大小：much 大很多、slightly 稍微高
❓ very better 對嗎？
錯，比較級前用 much / far，不用 very

6最高級：the + 最高級 + 範圍

one of the most + 複數
the + 最高級 + N + in / of / among + 範圍
結構例
the + 最高級the most reliable supplier in the company
one of the 最高級 + 複數one of the most efficient managers
找「範圍」判斷最高級：in the company / of the three / among all applicants / on the list。
❓ one of the most efficient ___ ：manager 還是 managers？
managers（one of the 後接複數）
❓ 看到 of the three 用哪一層？
最高級

7as … as 同等比較

中間放原級
as + 形容詞/副詞原級 + as ＝ 和……一樣……
狀態The room is as large as that one.
動作She responds as quickly as her supervisor.
陷阱：as more durable as → as durable as；as larger as → as large as。not as…as ＝ 不如（＝ more … than 反寫）。
❓ as ___ as 中間放原級還是比較級？
原級
❓ not as durable as ＝ ？
不如……耐用

8倍數比較

twice / three times as … as
倍數 + as + 原級 + as
可數as many orders as
不可數as much time as
中間仍是原級：twice as larger as → twice as large as。常見：twice / three times / half / double。
❓ twice as large as 中間為何不是 larger？
倍數比較中間放原級
❓ as ___ orders as：many 還是 much？
many（orders 可數）

9特殊比較片語

fewer/less · no…than · prior to
片語意思
fewer + 可數 / less + 不可數能數→fewer，不能數→less
no later than不晚於（最晚在…前）
no more than / no fewer than不超過 / 至少
prior / senior / superior / inferior + to接 to，不接 than
TOEIC 最常考 prior to（在……之前）：prior than / more prior to 都錯。
❓ fewer 還是 less time？
less（time 不可數）
❓ prior 後面接 to 還是 than？
to（prior to）

🔟絕對形容詞

本身已是最終狀態，不加 more/most
字錯對
uniquemore uniquetruly unique
perfectmost perfectnearly perfect
completemore completealmost complete
要修飾程度用 nearly / virtually / absolutely / truly / completely，不用 more / most。
❓ more unique 對嗎？
錯，unique 是絕對形容詞，用 truly unique

1️⃣1️⃣比較對象一致

that of / those of
單數 → that of ｜ 複數 → those of
不能拿 price 跟 company 比。higher than that company → higher than that of the other company。better than the old plan → better than those of the old plan（benefits 複數）。
❓ delivery time is shorter than ___：the competitors / that of our competitors？
that of our competitors（time 單數）
❓ benefits 比較後面用 that of 還是 those of？
those of（複數）

1️⃣2️⃣more than 的兩種意思

數字 vs 禮貌表達
用法意思
more than + 數字超過（more than 500 employees）
more than + happy/willing非常樂意（more than happy to help）
商業書信常見 more than happy to / more than willing to ＝ 非常樂意，不是比較。
❓ more than happy 是在比較嗎？
不是，是「非常樂意」的固定禮貌說法

🪤比較結構六大常見錯誤

more easier / very better
雙重比較級
規則：easier 已是比較級不加 more；very 不修飾比較級（用 much/far）
改正：The plan is more easier → easier；very better → much better
as more … as
as…as 放原級
規則：as…as 中間一定放原級，不放比較級
改正：as more durable as → as durable as
one of the most + 單數
單複數
規則：one of the + 最高級後面接複數名詞
改正：one of the most popular product → products
prior than
固定搭配
規則：prior/senior/superior/inferior 後面接 to，不接 than
改正：prior than Friday → prior to Friday
less + 可數名詞
fewer/less
規則：可數用 fewer，不可數用 less
改正：less employees → fewer employees
than that / those
比較對象一致
規則：單數 that of、複數 those of，且要和前面名詞一致
改正：benefits（複數）→ that of → those of

補充上課練習 A ｜ 先找比較線索

不要只說答案。先指出句中的線索，再說應該使用哪個結構。　點開看答案與線索。

1. The delivery service is ______ than it was last year.
faster　句中有 than，表示兩者比較，所以使用比較級 faster。
2. Ms. Wang is one of the most experienced ______ in the department.
managers　one of the most + 形容詞 + 複數名詞，所以使用複數 managers。
3. The updated software is as ______ as the previous version.
reliable　as + 形容詞原級 + as，所以使用原級 reliable，不能用 more reliable。
4. The automated system requires ______ employees and ______ time.
fewer employees / less time　employees 是可數名詞，用 fewer；time 是不可數名詞，用 less。
5. The cost of this service is lower than ______ of the premium plan.
that　比較的是單數名詞 cost，所以用 that 代替，避免重複。
6. Please complete the registration prior ______ attending the workshop.
to　固定搭配是 prior to + 名詞／V-ing，不能用 prior than。
補充上課練習 B ｜ 比較結構改錯

每題先說錯在哪裡，再說完整的正確句子。　點開看正確句子與說明。

1. The revised schedule is more easier to understand.
✔ The revised schedule is easier to understand.
easier 本身已經是比較級，不能再加 more。
2. This is one of the most popular product in our store.
✔ This is one of the most popular products in our store.
one of the 後面要接複數名詞 products。
3. The virtual meeting was as more productive as the face-to-face meeting.
✔ The virtual meeting was as productive as the face-to-face meeting.
as ... as 中間放形容詞原級，不放比較級。
4. Please submit all expense reports prior than Friday.
✔ Please submit all expense reports prior to Friday.
固定搭配是 prior to，不是 prior than。
5. The new branch needs less employees than the main office.
✔ The new branch needs fewer employees than the main office.
employees 是可數複數名詞，所以用 fewer。
6. The benefits of this plan are better than that of the previous plan.
✔ The benefits of this plan are better than those of the previous plan.
比較的是複數名詞 benefits，所以用 those，不用 that。
