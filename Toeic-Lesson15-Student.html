Secret Garden Condominium Complex

Secret Garden 住宅區介紹
Secret Garden Condominium Complex Less than a half hour by train to the Square, Secret Garden is known for its architecture. It is short on condos, but long on single-family houses ranging from the mundane to the magnificent. Secret Garden is a neighborhood with yards large enough to play catch and with neighbors friendly enough to join you. Upside Secret Garden truly is a village within the city, as its promoters say. It’s wrapped by a forest preserve, golf courses, and a country club. Side streets are quiet and tree-lined. Downside If you want a convenience store on every corner and an array of nightclubs, Secret Garden isn’t for you. Though the west side of Secret Garden is dotted with pubs, the neighborhood’s east side is dry. This is primarily a residential neighborhood, which means single people might find other Madison neighborhoods more vibrant. This can be a wonderful place for retirees.
