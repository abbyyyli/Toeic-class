Recommendation Letter · Questions 143–146

Recommendation for George Sweeney

George Sweeney 推薦信
Dear Mr. Bob Flinders, George Sweeney has requested that I write a letter of recommendation based on our professional association over the past three years. I’ve found Mr. Sweeney to be intelligent and trustworthy as well as energetic in carrying out his duties. I believe he will be an asset to any organization, so I would recommend him without reservations. I will be happy to provide any further information. Sincerely, Mary Dignen

親愛的 Bob Flinders 先生：

George Sweeney 請我根據我們過去三年的專業合作關係，為他寫一封推薦信。

我認為 Sweeney 先生聰明、值得信任，而且在履行職責時充滿活力。

我相信他會是任何組織的寶貴人才，因此我願意毫無保留地推薦他。

如需任何進一步資訊，我很樂意提供。

敬上，Mary Dignen

Announcement · Questions 157–158

River Green Clean-up Volunteers

River Green 週末河岸清理
Volunteers urged to support River Green clean-up in New Town at weekend Scores of volunteers are lining up to give the River Green in New Town a clean-up. Every year for the past five years, a tidy-up campaign has been organized along the riverside, and unsightly items in the water have been retrieved. This year’s effort is scheduled for Saturday, March 13th, from 10 a.m. until 2 p.m. In previous clean-ups, large items have been pulled out of the water, including bikes and shopping trolleys, and rubbish have been collected on the banks and towpaths. Andrea Waterman, who has been involved in the scheme for many years, said: “There will be three locations for volunteers to go to—Teaneck Riverside, outside the Madison corner, and the path outside The Riverside Bridge—and all are welcome. City council officers will give out gloves, pickers and plastic bags.”

呼籲志工本週末協助 New Town 的 River Green 河岸清理活動

許多志工正準備參與 River Green 的清理工作。過去五年，每年都會沿河岸舉辦一次整理活動，把水中有礙觀瞻的物品打撈出來。

今年的活動安排在 3 月 13 日星期六上午 10 點至下午 2 點舉行。

過去的清理活動曾從水中拉出腳踏車與購物推車等大型物品，也清除了河岸及拖船道上的垃圾。

多年來一直參與這項計畫的 Andrea Waterman 表示，志工可前往三個集合地點，所有人都歡迎參加。市議會人員將發放手套、夾取工具和塑膠袋。
