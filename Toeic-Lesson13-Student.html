Article · Questions 131–134

Customer Relationship Marketing

從銷售產品轉向顧客關係
Now people live in an age of choice. Customers are continually bombarded with purchasing alternatives in every aspect of their lives. Customers demand more and more from their purchases and their suppliers. Therefore, for companies, marketing must refocus away from selling products and toward creating relationships. Nowadays companies direct their attention toward creating and maintaining an ongoing customer relationship.

現今，人們生活在一個充滿選擇的時代。

顧客在生活的每一個層面，都不斷接收到各種購買選項的資訊。

顧客對購買的商品以及供應商提出越來越高的要求。

因此，對企業而言，行銷重點必須從銷售產品轉向建立關係。

現今，企業把注意力轉向建立並維持持續的顧客關係。

Article · Questions 135–138

Growing Up and Handling Frustration

成長中的灰色地帶與挫折容忍
As children grow and mature, they learn that life is not black-or-white but many shades of gray. They experience a series of ups and downs, where sometimes their parents make the right decisions and sometimes they don’t. Children can grow up to be adults who demand that life be consistent and predictable. One of the most important qualities for children to develop is the ability to tolerate frustration.

隨著孩子成長成熟，他們會明白人生並非非黑即白，而是存在許多灰色地帶。

他們會經歷一連串起起落落；父母有時做出正確決定，有時則沒有。

孩子可能長大成為要求人生必須一致且可預測的成年人。

孩子需要培養的最重要特質之一，是容忍挫折的能力。

Professional Sales Conference Invitation

邀請專業銷售會議的提醒信
Dear Mr. James, I am writing to check if you received my e-mail of August 13th inviting you to speak at the Professional Sales conference to be held on October 21st. We are interested in having you share your experience with the audience. If you did not receive my e-mail or you would like additional information, please call me at 123-3382-3849. Your early reply will be highly appreciated as we expect to send the conference agenda to the printers by the end of this week. Sincerely, Paul Moore

親愛的 James 先生：

我寫信是想確認您是否收到我 8 月 13 日的電子郵件；信中邀請您在 10 月 21 日舉行的專業銷售會議上發言。

我們很希望您能與聽眾分享您的經驗。

如果您沒有收到我的電子郵件，或想取得更多資訊，請撥打 123-3382-3849 與我聯絡。

由於我們預計在本週結束前把會議議程送交印刷廠，若您能儘早回覆，我們將非常感激。

敬上，Paul Moore
